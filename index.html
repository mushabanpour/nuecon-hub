Datasets you can use

What Northeastern has licensed, and the shortest route to an account. Drawn from Key Resources for Economics PhD Research at NU and the library’s guides, checked 9 October 2026.

Licensed research data — apply for an account

These need a registration and at least one approval step, so start well before you need the data.

From the guide

NielsenIQ via the Kilts Center

Retail Scanner (weekly store-level prices and volumes, 2006–), Consumer Panel (40–60k households, 2004–), Ad Intel, PromoData, PanelView and Ailments & Health surveys.

  1. Open the Kilts registration page; choose “United States” and “Northeastern University”.
  2. Confirm the institutional-contract prompt, then fill in the Request Access form (role, advisor, graduation date).
  3. Your advisor approves, then the NU Kilts contact; Kilts emails you to describe your project, accept terms and pick datasets.

The Kilts page lists tenured/tenure-track faculty, PhD students and postdocs as eligible.

Kilts registration ↗
From the guide

Wharton Research Data Services (WRDS)

About 100 subscribed products as of Aug 2024, incl. Compustat, CRSP, WRDS Environmental (EPA ECHO panels), MSCI ESG, NYSE TAQ. Includes WRDS Cloud for SSH/batch jobs.

  1. Register with your @northeastern.edu email; subscriber is “Northeastern University – D’Amore-McKim School of Business”, user type Ph.D.
  2. The NU WRDS contact approves the request; accept the Terms of Use.
  3. WRDS Cloud is included for PhD accounts (not class or day-pass accounts); Stata there needs a separate agreement.

Subscription list was described as of August 2024 in the guide; it may have changed.

WRDS registration ↗
NU access (per department)

Dewey Data

One platform for 30+ commercial providers: foot traffic and points of interest (e.g. SafeGraph, Advan), mobility, consumer transactions, real estate (ATTOM), web traffic, job postings and wages.

  1. Go to the Dewey platform and sign up or sign in with your @northeastern.edu address (other universities use SSO this way).
  2. Accept the academic, non-commercial terms; some datasets carry extra partner terms.
  3. Filter before downloading and keep download scripts separate from analysis.

I could not find a public Northeastern page describing the sign-in route. If your email is not recognized, ask the library (library_ask@northeastern.edu).

Computing power

When a regression takes two hours on your laptop, move it here.

From the guide

Discovery cluster (Research Computing)

Free high-performance computing for Northeastern students, housed at the MGHPCC in Holyoke. The guide reports 50,000+ CPU cores and 525+ GPUs; you can ask for up to 128 CPUs (12 with a GPU), up to 128 GB of memory and 24-hour sessions in JupyterLab.

  1. Submit the Research Computing Access Request and choose your advisor as “University sponsor”.
  2. After approval, sign in to Open OnDemand in Chrome, Firefox or Edge (Safari and phones are not supported).
  3. Pick an app — JupyterLab, RStudio, MATLAB, Stata, SAS, SPSS — then set partition (short, long, gpu), CPUs, memory and time.

Research Computing’s current pages also refer to an “Explorer” cluster at ood.explorer.northeastern.edu. Check the Open OnDemand page for the current address before you bookmark it.

Access request ↗Open OnDemand info ↗Get help ↗
From the guide

WRDS Cloud

Linux grid next to the WRDS data, reached over SSH. Run SAS, R, Python, MATLAB (Stata only with a separate agreement) interactively or as batch jobs.

  1. Comes with a WRDS PhD account; connect with SSH from your terminal.
